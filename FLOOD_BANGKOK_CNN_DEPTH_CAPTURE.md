# CNN versus historical reports: recorded-depth capture

For the **six approximately located Bang Kapi BMA road sites** with October–December 2025 flood reports, the total of recorded depths across **eight site-days** is **115 cm**. At the same **25-cell inspection budget** as the earlier backtest, the pre-October CNN and recent-report history each select **four sites / four site-days**, with **60 cm of summed reported depth**. At **10 cells**, the CNN selects three sites worth **45 cm**, versus two sites worth **30 cm** for history.

The sum of centimetres is a **depth-weighted event count**: it adds separate flood reports and is **not** one water depth, flood volume, or affected area. The CNN predicts complaint occurrence, not water depth. This six-site retrospective check is too small to establish a general severity advantage.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 3 / 6 | **45 / 115 cm** | 2 / 6 | **30 / 115 cm** |
| **25** | **4 / 6** | **60 / 115 cm** | **4 / 6** | **60 / 115 cm** |

At 25 cells, both queues include the same four sites: Ramkhamhaeng Soi 43 (20 cm), Bang Kapi intersection (15 cm), Ramkhamhaeng Soi 33 (15 cm), and Ramkhamhaeng Soi 20 (10 cm). Both miss Hua Mak / Ramkhamhaeng 24 (15 cm on each of two days) and Lam Sali (10 and 15 cm on two days). At 10 cells, the CNN additionally includes Soi 33. The **deepest recorded Bang Kapi site, 20 cm, is captured by both methods** even at 10 cells.

## Fair timing and limits

The local 2D CNN was trained on Bang Kapi 250 m cell complaint-buffer labels from **2022–31 July 2025**; checkpoint selection and calibration used **August–September 2025**. Its queue was scored using **40 rainy October–December days from 2022–2024**, with report history frozen on 30 September 2025. **No October–December 2025 rain or reports enter this queue.** The historical rule counts report-positive days over the preceding 12 months. The retrained CNN selects the same epoch (13) and August–September AP (0.03197) as the earlier local CNN experiment.

The BMA rows provide [recorded road-flood depths](https://data.bangkok.go.th/dataset/frd_dds) but no event coordinates. The six site cells use BMA's **2026 flood-risk inventory**, published after the 2025 floods, so their 2025 positions are approximate. These sites cover only a small part of the [11-district depth audit](FLOOD_BANGKOK_DEPTH_AUDIT.md). They cannot support a claim about CNN depth capture across all 11 districts or about unseen sites.

The script also writes the selected CNN checkpoint for reproducibility.
