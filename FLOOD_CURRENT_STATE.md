# Bangkok flood modeling: current state and experiment ledger

**As of 24 October 2026.** Read this page first. The project has built useful **rankings of reported problem sites** and of **satellite-mapped water**. It has **not** validated a map of actual flood probability, depth, or what would flood under twice as much rain. These are three different targets; their percentages must not be pooled.

**Drainage-data correction, 23 October 2026:** a newly identified public [BMA ArcGIS drainage service](FLOOD_BANGKOK_DRAINAGE_GIS.md) exposes **81,574 geocoded pipe polylines** plus canal, gate, pump and tunnel layers. The earlier BMA pipe CSV alone lacked coordinates. A Bang Kapi audit found 2,454 pipe lines and 129 *candidate* pipe–canal connection clusters. The GIS geometry is from 2021 and does not verify inlet/outfall operation, historical pump/gate state, or flood extent. A [new citywide static network-feature test](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_BMA_DRAINAGE_NETWORK_TEST.md) uses these pipes but does not show a consistent ranking gain.

**Surface-flow pilot, 24 October 2026:** a [Lat Krabang 10 m crossing experiment](FLOOD_LAT_KRABANG_EDGE_FLOW_PILOT.md) uses terrain-derived candidate passes and controlled rain to rank GISTDA-positive mapped water at 250 m. On a 20 October exploratory event holdout, the selected scenario's top-10% cells capture **32.86%** of mapped water versus **23.93%** for ordinary mean-elevation flow, **25.40%** for reversed flow, and **79.37%** for crop cover. The physical gain is scenario-dependent and does not establish urban depth or probability.

**Traffy transfer check, 24 October 2026:** in **999** terrain-covered Lat Krabang cells, the frozen masked CNN captures **11.37%** of **35** first-in-prior-year Q4 2025 report-positive cell-days at the same 10% local queue budget; the candidate-pass flow score captures **2.86–5.71%** across three rain scenarios. January–September report validation selects the unchanged CNN. This local score is not comparable with the earlier citywide ~14% queue. [Matched transfer and target-mix audit](FLOOD_LAT_KRABANG_TRAFFY_TRANSFER.md).

**Data-count curve, 24 October 2026:** a [district-by-district chart](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_DATA_COUNT_CAPTURE_CURVE.md) compares nearby-history ranking with the ~14% masked-CNN-plus-ordinary-path model. With a separate daily top-10% cell budget in each district, model lift is **+0.73 points** in the lower-history 25 districts and **+2.76 points** in the higher-history 25, but the high-minus-low interval spans zero and district-level history count versus lift has Spearman **−0.067**. More local report data does not show a reliable monotonic model advantage.

**District uplift audit, 24 October 2026:** [site-level diagnostics](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_DELTA_PATTERN.md) find a stronger descriptive relation with **report density per cell** than raw report count: the lower-density 25 districts gain **+3.77 points** versus **−0.10** in the higher-density 25. About **89%** of net extra captures are adjacent to prior-year report cells; only **1 of 79** novel positives beyond three side-neighbor steps is captured by the model. The density split was examined after Q4 and is exploratory, while the 100 m complaint buffer can make adjacent cells reflect the same physical report site.

**Physical-site target follow-up, 24 October 2026:** [raw Traffy coordinate clustering and site-level CNN training](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_PHYSICAL_SITE_TEST.md) replace the buffered-cell "new" label with a first-in-year reported-site proxy. At a 250 m site radius, the source-selected model captures **10.01% of 471 Q4 site events** in daily district queues versus **5.68%** for nearby history and **7.10%** for the earlier CNN, with a fixed 10% total cell budget and 20% of slots reserved for zero-own-history cells. The lift over history is +4.34 points; the gain over the earlier CNN remains uncertain. A synthetic 3×3 repeat-site mask does not consistently help. Only one of 43 sites more than 750 m from any prior-year report is captured by the new model. The 250 m target radius and Q4 2025 evaluation remain exploratory, not an untouched prospective test.

**Time-matched rain follow-up, 24 October 2026:** a [matched hourly-rain CNN experiment](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_SITE_RAIN_HOURLY.md) adds five pre-cutoff rain channels to a site-trained CNN. In six-hour district queues it captures **8.34%** of the same 471 new-site events versus **7.64%** for its matched no-rain CNN; a one-hour timing check reverses to **8.04% versus 8.12%**. The six-hour gain is small and uneven across folds, and the one-hour check does not repeat it. The prior daily **10.01%** result uses another queue setup, so it is not a matched comparator. Rain intensity remains a plausible input, but this test does not show a stable ranking improvement or validate flood scenarios.

**What “capture” means:** within each day, select the highest-ranked 10% of candidate cells and count what share of known *positive report cell-days* falls there. It does **not** measure false alarms, precision, all flooded ground, or a calibrated probability. All Q4 2025 comparisons below are **exploratory** because that quarter has been inspected repeatedly during this project.

**Counting correction, 24 October 2026:** a previous 8-bit dot product undercounted first-in-a-year positives on seven busy January–September source-validation fold-days (**11,924** instead of **13,716** across five overlapping source folds). The affected helpers now use a safe sum. Choices in the new connected-path and continuity-loss tests do not change because arms within each fold share the same denominator. The Q4 held-out first-in-a-year count is **1,664** with either method; the Q4 results for these tests are unaffected. Older artifacts with source-validation percentages have not all been rebuilt. [Audit in connected-path test](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_CONNECTED_SCORE_SMOOTHER.md).

The latest monotone rain-response fit was also tested with **eight districts excluded from response-weight fitting and selection**. Within those 6,579 candidate cells, their Q4 queue captured **60.23%** of 978 report-positive cell-days with the **recent + nearby history formula**, **60.84%** with the uphill path, **60.94%** with local built × rain, and **62.16%** with the distance control. The largest gain over the *same eight-district history queue* is therefore **1.93 percentage points**, about 19 additional positive cell-days; its seven-day-block 95% interval is **−1.57 to +4.27 points**. Earlier report history in the held-out districts remains available to every score, so this is a test of the learned response's geographic transfer, not a cold start. The 60% scores are not comparable with the 47.18% citywide **raw 365-day count**, or with the 48.17% citywide **recent + nearby formula**, because the candidate area and top-10% queue differ. The unconstrained distance diagnostic reached 48.69% citywide, but learned a negative peak-rain response; it is not the recommended rain-response model. [Full path and holdout results](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_UPHILL_PAVED_BACKTEST.md).

A later **learned-history transfer test** fitted shared recency and multiscale-neighborhood weights on other districts. A loss aimed at the daily top-10% cutoff raised capture in the original eight held-out districts from **60.23% to 63.69%**, but across five separate geographic folds its pooled queue captured **47.10% versus 47.11%** for the fixed recent + nearby formula; the exploratory difference interval is **−0.97 to +1.00 points**. Adding physical static features did not consistently help. The fixed formula remains the supported benchmark. [Learned-history transfer](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_LEARNED_HISTORY_TRANSFER.md).

A **spatial CNN correction** to that fixed score gives a more promising, still exploratory result. After choosing a small residual multiplier on source-district validation queues, it captured **47.96% versus 47.11%** for the fixed formula across five separate geographic-fold queues, about **61 additional Q4 report-positive cell-days**. Every fold improved, but the seven-day-block interval for the pooled difference is **−0.48 to +2.16 points**; the original eight-district holdout separately scored **60.11% versus 60.23%**. One citywide queue scored **49.71% versus 48.17%** but includes the districts used to fit the CNN. Keep the fixed formula as the operational benchmark until a new season confirms the CNN gain. [CNN transfer test](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_HISTORY_CNN_TRANSFER.md).

A follow-up **center-masked multiscale CNN** forced the learned branch to use neighboring cells only and trained against the actual full source-district daily top-10% cutoff. On the **same five held-out queues**, it scored **47.954%**, effectively tied with the earlier CNN's **47.962%** and above the fixed score's **47.113%**. It captured **zero of 1,664** positive cell-days with no own-cell report in the prior year at that queue budget; the earlier CNN captured about three. Its same-day adjacent-cell cluster capture was **61.677%** versus **61.508%** for fixed, a small proxy gain. This architecture does not solve finding newly reporting sites, so the fixed history formula remains the practical benchmark. [Masked multiscale CNN test](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_MASKED_MULTISCALE_CNN.md).

The **separate first-in-a-year queue** was then tested with a simple nearby-history score and a denser CNN using terrain, cover, road and canal inputs. Reserving 20% of the **same inspection slots** for zero-own-history cells captured **11.946%** of their 1,664 Q4 positives with the simple score, versus **9.887%** with the CNN. Overall capture fell from **47.113%** with no reserve to **44.114%** and **43.639%**, respectively. Source-district validation selected **no reserve in all five folds** for the objective of total report capture. Denser convolutions and random historical-input masking did not demonstrate an advantage over simple neighbor history. [Spatial novel-site queue](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_SPATIAL_NOVEL_QUEUE.md).

Pushing the masking approach further, **synthetically hiding prior own-cell reports at repeat sites** raised the novel-slot CNN's pooled Q4 capture at the same 20% reserve. Adding random 3 × 3 history blocks reached **13.529%** first-in-a-year capture and **44.479%** overall, versus **11.946%** and **44.114%** for the simple neighbor score. However, selecting the mask arm on source-district validation produced only **12.178%** and **44.168%**; its exploratory intervals versus simple include zero. The full block-mask Q4 gain is a post hoc comparison, and the no-reserve fixed queue still wins total capture. [Mask augmentation test](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_SPATIAL_MASK_AUGMENTATION.md).

A later **rank blend of that masked CNN and nearby history** reached **13.582%** first-in-a-year capture with blend weights chosen on source districts, only about one extra positive cell-day over the CNN alone. Its gain over the CNN has a date-block interval that includes zero. Restricting the CNN's hard comparison pool from the highest 20% to the highest 5% of nearby-history scores cut capture to **6.237%**; source validation rejected the change in every fold. Splitting the same novel reserve between the original CNN for adjacent-history cells and a target-centered CNN for isolated cells reached **13.469%** with source-selected slot shares. The 15–20% target has not been met on the same 1,664-positive queue. The CNN arms themselves had previously been inspected on Q4, so these follow-ups remain exploratory. [Masked CNN follow-up](FLOOD_MASK_CNN_NEXT_STEP.md).

A **daily queue-loss continuation** then trained that same masked CNN to put actual first-in-a-year training positives above a smooth, fixed-capacity novel-slot cutoff. A matched pairwise continuation used the same source days, positives, initialization, optimizer and epochs. January–September source validation retained the original checkpoint in all five queue-loss folds; it selected one updated pairwise fold, whose Q4 capture fell slightly. Pooled first-in-a-year Q4 capture was **13.529%** with the source-selected queue-loss arm (entirely the original model) and **13.482%** with the source-selected pairwise arm. This loss did not move the result toward 15%. [Queue-loss follow-up](FLOOD_MASK_CNN_NEXT_STEP.md).

The [interactive Q4 queue map](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_Q4_QUEUE_MAP.md) overlays report-positive cells on the nearby-history and masked-CNN selections, with a top-area slider, date and district controls, and a different-picks view. Its default 10% area reproduces the same five-fold Q4 metrics, including fractional ties.

A **one-hop continuity check** blended that saved masked CNN's scores with side-adjacent cells, using either BMA DTM mean-height differences or equal neighbor weights. Blend strength was selected on January–September source districts. On the same Q4 first-in-a-year queue, terrain-weighted smoothing captured **14.083%** versus **13.529%** for the original CNN; ordinary neighbor smoothing captured **14.113%**. The terrain gain over the original has an exploratory seven-day-block interval of **−0.55 to +1.28 percentage points**, and terrain does not beat ordinary smoothing. The map can outline *possible* adjacent extent around reported cells, but those outlined cells are neither observed wet labels nor model inputs. Mean elevation at 250 m cannot verify an unobstructed water path. [Continuity test and map](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_TERRAIN_CONTINUITY.md).

A follow-up **one-to-three-hop connected-path smoother** passed masked-CNN scores through side-adjacent cells, including cells with prior reports as bridges. Source-district validation chose path length and blending. On the same held-out Q4 queue, ordinary paths captured **14.263%** of first-in-a-year positives versus **13.529%** for the saved CNN. Directional terrain paths captured **13.685%**; terrain plus OSM building footprint and WorldCover vegetation proxies captured **13.642%**. A reversed-terrain control reached **14.603%**, so the data do not support interpreting this as learned or verified water flow. A variant that let only high-CNN-score intermediate cells relay reached **13.685%** with terrain/building/cover, versus **13.462%** for an equally gated ordinary graph and **14.263%** for ungated ordinary paths. The physical graph's interval versus ordinary paths includes zero (**−1.28 to +0.26 percentage points**). These are report rankings, not flood extent. [Connected-path smoother](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_CONNECTED_SCORE_SMOOTHER.md).

A separate **continuity-loss probe** fine-tuned the masked CNN with a soft penalty when three plausible incoming neighbors scored above a low center cell. In a matched one-epoch continuation on 90 sampled training days per fold, the terrain-loss arm captured **13.510%** of first-in-a-year Q4 reports, versus **13.362%** for a no-loss continuation and **13.529%** for the original checkpoint. Source validation chose no terrain-loss arm in all five folds. The loss operates on uncalibrated report-ranking logits and cannot establish physically continuous flood extent. [Continuity-loss probe](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_CONTINUITY_LOSS.md).

An **adjacent-history leakage check** found that 99.94% of 2023–2024 synthetic repeat positives had a prior report in one of the eight neighboring cells, compared with 74.63% of genuine first-in-a-year positives. In a matched five-fold test, moving the same 3 × 3 training mask from a random patch location to the target's immediate neighborhood reduced Q4 first-in-a-year capture **13.044% → 10.184%** (paired date-block interval for centered minus random: **−4.62 to −1.31 points**). The centered model found **10 of 332** positives with no adjacent prior-year report versus zero for random-mask and simple-neighbor rankings, but missed more of the 1,332 with adjacent history. Source-selected mask location gave **12.171%** novel capture, not a demonstrated gain over simple nearby history. Thus the older synthetic-mask improvement primarily reflects use of nearby report history, while true isolated-site transfer remains weak. [Target-site mask leakage test](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_TARGET_SITE_MASK_LEAKAGE.md).

A later **joint satellite + urban CNN mask test** trained matched centered/random arms and urban-only controls while withholding Pathum Thani satellite scenes and Bangkok EOS labels. Across **three seeds**, outside the two urban training patches, centered masking captured **15, 4, and 6** of 220 Q4 report-positive cells with no own or adjacent prior-year complaint; matched random masking captured **1, 3, and 6**. The large first-seed gain is unstable. Across all **959** first-in-a-year positives there, centered captured **105, 80, and 104**, below random's **114, 82, and 113**. Satellite auxiliary training's urban gain also changes sign across seeds. On held-out GISTDA and EOS mapped-water rankings, centered masking is worse than random in all three seeds, and neither arm consistently beats crop cover. The fixed no-reserve history queue still leads total urban report capture. The satellite heads do not consume masked report history directly, so this is an indirect shared-encoder test, not direct satellite-history masking. [Joint mask holdouts](FLOOD_JOINT_TARGET_SITE_MASK_HOLDOUT.md).

A **direct isolated-site training test** then fitted a separate CNN and tabular ranker on actual report-positive cells with no own or eight-adjacent prior-year report, grouping touching same-day cells before training. There were **767** such groups in 2023–2024. At the same 20%-of-slots isolated reserve across five held-out district queues, the simple **750 m/1.5 km/3 km distant-history score** captured **65 / 332 (19.58%)** isolated Q4 positive cell-days; the CNN captured **54.34–60.34 / 332 (16.37–18.18%)** across three seeds, and the tabular model **26–27.6 / 332**. The CNN's group capture crossed the simple score in only one seed. Source validation favored CNN group capture in 14 of 15 fold-seed comparisons, so the combined geographic/seasonal holdout exposes a transfer gap. Overall capture with the isolated reserve is about **42.1–42.3%**, below the **47.11%** no-reserve fold queue. Keep distant history as the isolated-exploration benchmark; none of these are verified flood probabilities. [Isolated-site training](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_ISOLATED_SITE_RANKER.md).

A follow-up **multiscale spatial residual CNN** supplied the isolated-site model with 750 m, 1.5 km and 3 km pre-day report-history layers and trained a correction to the fixed distant-history score. On the same five geographic folds and 20%-of-slots isolated reserve, the fixed score captured **65 / 332** isolated positive cell-days and **44 / 158** touching groups. Across three seeds, source-validation-selected CNN correction captured **66, 59 and 65** cells and **38, 40 and 42** groups. A small fixed correction added a few cells but did not consistently improve groups; Q4 remains exploratory. The wider input removes a receptive-field mismatch, but does **not** demonstrate transferable learned spatial value. [Multiscale isolated-site test](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_ISOLATED_CONTEXT_RESIDUAL.md).

A **group-level loss with explicit target-history masks** was then tried. On the same first-in-a-year queue as the earlier **13.529%** historical-repeat masked CNN, it captured **11.602%, 11.659% and 10.697%** across three seeds, below both that CNN and simple nearby history (**11.946%**). The target center history was zeroed symmetrically for all positive-group members and unreported comparisons; historical repeat groups lost their actual own history. The group model found **1, 1 and 3 of 332** truly isolated positives versus zero for the older masked CNN, but lost many adjacent-history sites and groups. A separate stricter isolated-site test masked the full 3 × 3 target history **and its contributions to smoothed history channels**: source-selected masked-adjacent group models captured **59.6, 64 and 54.8 of 332** isolated positive cells versus **65** for the fixed distant-history rule; their group hits were **45, 46 and 38.8 versus 44**. No stable spatial-context gain is demonstrated. [Group loss and target-mask tests](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_GROUP_TARGET_MASK.md).

A **synthetic LiDAR downhill-routing pretrain** was then tested on the same strict isolated-site queue. The CNN learned some synthetic target structure in excluded districts (synthetic Spearman **0.305–0.539** across folds), and improved source-district January–September 2025 isolated capture (**15.25%** versus **13.52%** for a matched scratch LiDAR CNN and **13.20%** for distant history). On excluded-district Q4 it captured only **50/332 cells and 31/158 touching groups**, versus **48/332 and 30/158** for scratch and **65/332 and 44/158** for distant history. Thus the auxiliary task did not give useful geographic/seasonal transfer. Its synthetic D8 target omits actual urban drainage and is not verified flooding. [Route pretraining](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_ROUTE_PRETRAIN.md).

A stronger **direction-aware route loss** supervised eight incoming-neighbor fluxes and the nine-class outgoing downhill neighbor/sink, as well as total arriving runoff. On excluded districts it learned the synthetic outgoing D8 direction at **25–35% top-1 accuracy**, above the **15–19%** most-common-class baseline. But its Q4 report queue found only **46/332** isolated positive cells and **32/158** touching groups, versus **65/332** and **44/158** for distant history. Learning a terrain direction rule did not improve report-site transfer. [Direction-aware routing loss](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_DIRECTION_ROUTE_LOSS.md).

A **matched-path spatial pretrain** then ranked a synthetic source connected by a 2–6-hop LiDAR D8 route above an equally distant, elevation-matched unconnected source at the same target cell. Held-out synthetic pair accuracy was **62.4%** pooled, falling to **53.5%** with LiDAR input removed: the CNN learned some spatial terrain relation. Yet its Q4 strict isolated report queue found **40/332** positive cells with the pretraining head kept, or **42.78/332** with that head reset, versus **48/332** for a scratch LiDAR CNN and **65/332** for distant history. Only **13/332** positive cells even had a prior-year report on one of these D8 paths, while **323/332** had a prior report within a broad six-cell square. The learned source-to-target relation is rarely present in the report task; training it harder did not establish useful transfer. [Matched-path pretraining and coverage](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_PATH_CONTRAST_PRETRAIN.md).

The path-pretrained model was subsequently **fine-tuned with exact 3 × 3 target-history masking**, including Gaussian-history correction, and compared with a matched masked CNN trained from scratch. With source-only correction selection, pretrained versus scratch isolated Q4 cell hits were **62.78 vs 53**, **68.78 vs 63**, and **60.34 vs 64** across three fine-tuning seeds, while distant history found **65/332** in each. The pretraining gain reversed in the third seed; mean pretrained capture (**63.97/332**) remained below history, and mean group capture did not improve over scratch. This is a mixed, exploratory transfer signal, not a stable win on emerging report sites. [Path pretraining plus target mask](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_PATH_PRETRAIN_TARGET_MASK.md).

A later **BMA road-corridor weak-supervision stage** pretrained that same path CNN on uncertain named-road bags before the exact target-mask Traffy fit. Across three matched seeds, real BMA labels found **70, 71.78, and 64.78 of 332** isolated Q4 positive cells, versus path-only **62.78, 68.78, and 60.34** and distant history **65**. Touching-group hits were **44, 51.78, and 47.78 of 158**, versus path-only **41, 46.78, and 45.34**. A half-pair road-label shuffle scored lower in all three runs. Mean real-minus-path cell capture was **+1.47 percentage points**, with an exploratory seven-day-block interval of **−0.62 to +2.63**; the model still fell just below history in one seed. This is promising but not a stable operational win. A matched BMA road is a corridor, not a verified wet cell. [Road-corridor pretraining](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_BMA_ROAD_BAGS.md).

The newly found **BMA pipe GIS was then tested as a static graph-derived correction** to that frozen road model. We snapped pipe endpoints within 5 m and measured undirected path distance to 4,071 name-and-proximity-matched *candidate* canal connections, alongside pipe, pump and gate proximity. A three-layer CNN learned the correction from source-district 2023–2024 isolated groups. On the same held-out Q4 queues, source-selected real GIS corrections found **71, 68.78 and 61 of 332** isolated cells across the three seeds, versus the frozen model's **70, 71.78 and 64.78**. Group hits were **45, 48.78 and 44 of 158**, versus **44, 51.78 and 47.78**. The real links beat shuffled candidate locations on average but failed to improve the frozen model in two seeds. Only **4,603/25,812** cells have a candidate within 5 km through the approximated pipe graph. These links are unverified and have no operating state; do not promote the GIS correction on this result. [Pipe-network test](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_BMA_DRAINAGE_NETWORK_TEST.md).

A **general spatial physics pretraining stage** then used 20 Thai SRTM/OSM/WorldCover patches and held out Bang Kapi, Nong Chok, Chiang Mai city and Hat Yai city before any report-mask training. Across three seeds, a constrained spatial CNN imitated a *heuristic synthetic* one-step water-transfer teacher on held-out cells with **0.665 mm** mean surface error, versus **0.965 mm** for a local-only constrained model and **1.638 mm** for an untrained constrained network. In a four-step rollout with doubled synthetic rain, errors were **2.537, 3.375 and 8.070 mm**, respectively. Spatial beat local-only in all 12 seed–place comparisons for both scenarios. This shows transfer of the synthetic spatial task, **not** validated flood extent or rain simulation. [Pretraining audit](FLOOD_THAI_SPATIAL_PHYSICS_PRETRAIN.md). A later masked-report transfer is a separate outcome and should not be inferred from these errors.

A [10 m local-inertial surface-flow solver](FLOOD_SURFACE_LOCAL_INERTIAL_TEACHER.md) now supplies a more explicit **candidate synthetic teacher** with a defined time step, Manning friction, face barriers and exact cell-to-cell water accounting. It passed lake-at-rest, pulse, rain, barrier and time-step-refinement checks on a Bang Kapi LiDAR crop. It has **not yet** replaced the 250 m heuristic teacher in the multi-place training result above, and its closed-boundary, no-drainage demo depths are not observed flood depths.

The official **SFINCS** solver now runs on the same Bang Kapi crop as the local-inertial numerical control. Under matched closed-edge, zero-drainage rain scenarios, mean cell depth differences were **0.38 mm** at 30 mm/h and **0.52 mm** at 60 mm/h after 15 minutes, with near-exact rain volume storage. This is solver agreement, not observed flood accuracy. [Simulator trial](FLOOD_SIMULATOR_SELECTION.md).

A follow-up [SFINCS physics pretraining stage](FLOOD_SFINCS_PHYSICS_PRETRAIN.md) generated **270** Bang Kapi LiDAR tile–storm cases and taught 10 m spatial CNNs to predict the next 5-minute SFINCS depth map. On 18 geographically withheld tiles, a plain dilated CNN's final 15-minute rollout error was **3.45 mm**, versus **3.60 mm** for U-Net and **8.94 mm** for a matched local-only network. With both those tiles and a 60 mm/h, 20-minute storm withheld, errors were **6.86, 7.49 and 15.84 mm**, respectively. The output conserves whole-tile water by construction. **U-Net did not beat the simpler spatial CNN.** This establishes transferable **simulator imitation within one LiDAR footprint**, not real flood accuracy or improvement to the separate 250 m Bangkok report CNN; the architectures and physical scales differ.

Averaging the three path-pretrained masked CNN residual maps also failed to clear history. Source-district validation chose a `0.25` correction in all five folds, but the held-out Q4 queue found only **60.34/332 isolated cells and 42.34/158 groups** versus history's **65 and 44**. A weaker `0.1` setting nearly tied cell capture (**64.84**) but was not selected without Q4. The bottleneck includes validation-to-test shift in correction strength, beyond seed noise. [Ensemble artifact](artifacts/flood_open_weak_train/multi_target/citywide/q4_2025_path_mask_three_seed_ensemble_fivefold.json).

A **joint target-type follow-up** added pre-Q4 UNOSAT mapped-water supervision through a separate satellite input/head and the same spatial steps used by the target-masked Traffy report ranker. It excluded Bangkok and Pathum Thani satellite scenes from fitting and omitted post-Q4 GISTDA/EOS labels. Across three seeds, source-selected path-joint isolated Q4 hits were **72.8, 56.78, and 58 of 332**, versus path report-only **62.78, 68.78, and 60.34** and simple distant history **65**. A same-step control with spatially shuffled satellite labels found **65, 69, and 52.78**; real-label minus shuffled gain changed sign across seeds. The satellite head learned mapped water on held-out Pathum Thani but was around the crop-cover baseline. This does **not** demonstrate transferable spatial-context value for emerging Bangkok report sites. [Joint path-mask target training](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_JOINT_PATH_MASK.md).

## Practical reading of the project

1. **For a useful hackathon prototype now:** show a **report-site priority map** powered by recent + nearby complaint history, with known BMA road sites and terrain/cover/canal context as inspectable layers. Label it as a *report-risk ranking*. If making a frozen pre-season list, compare it with the simple recent-report rule at the same budget.
2. **For model research:** retain surface cover and mapped canal geometry as candidates, but require gains over history, local cover, equal-reach distance, and no-edge/reverse-path controls on new districts and storms. Also measure sites with no own-cell report history separately; the current CNN corrections miss them at the top-10% budget. The uphill-path and new BMA pipe-feature experiments did not clear that bar.
3. **For a rain-responsive flood map:** obtain time-matched **urban wet and dry observations** and drainage state, then evaluate extent and scenario response on unseen storms. Feeding doubled rain into today's CNN, U-Net, tree, or uphill ranker changes a score; it does not establish what would actually flood. [Scenario stress test](FLOOD_BANGKOK_SCENARIO_PREPARATION.md), [verified-extent plan](FLOOD_BANGKOK_VERIFIED_EXTENT_PLAN.md).

The main failure pattern is **target mismatch and missing observation state**, followed by uncertain drainage connections and poorly calibrated counts. More training patches or a larger network have not overcome those gaps in the measured tests above.
