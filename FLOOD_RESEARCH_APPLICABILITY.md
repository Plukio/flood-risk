# Applying the research roadmap to this Bangkok project

**22 October 2026.** The supplied research report describes useful methods, but its recommended models are not evidence that this project's current outputs predict inundation. Start from the [current experiment ledger](FLOOD_CURRENT_STATE.md): the strongest citywide result is a ranking of future **Traffy report-positive cells**, while verified street-flood extent, depth, and doubled-rain outcomes remain open questions.

## What we can use now

| Research recommendation | Project evidence | Practical application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audit the experiment ledger and add stronger MLOps | The ledger already separates report, satellite-water, and depth targets. Q4 2025 has been examined repeatedly. | Keep a result record with target meaning, source and time, observed/unknown mask, geographic and storm split, baseline, metric, code/data version, and limitations. The new [label readiness audit](flood_label_audit.py) validates all 205 cached weak-label scenes and records file hashes. A tracking server or registry becomes useful when repeated prospective runs need coordinated model promotion; it does not solve weak labels. |
| Use citizen reports for ground truth | Traffy provides geolocated positive *problem reports* and the recent-history score captures **48.17%** of Q4 2025 report-positive cell-days in the top 10% of Bangkok cells. Reporting depends on people, location, and access; no report does not mean dry. | Use Traffy for a clearly named **report-site priority queue**, and as corroboration for independently observed water. Preserve original report time, location uncertainty, duplicate status, and provenance. Review photos before treating them as visible water evidence. [History test](FLOOD_CITYWIDE_HISTORY_RANKER.md). |
| Use satellite radar to map water | GISTDA, EOS-RS and UNOSAT products are already in the project. GISTDA's Bangkok target is mostly crop-area water; EOS-RS and GISTDA transparent pixels have no dry/valid mask. Only UNOSAT supplies an analysis extent that can support *weak* nonwater comparisons. | Trace source scene IDs and acquisition times, obtain valid-observation masks, separate permanent water from temporary floodwater, and evaluate built surfaces separately. Never turn transparent pixels into dry labels. [Open data audit](FLOOD_OPEN_DATA_AUDIT_2026.md); [data priorities](FLOOD_NEXT_DATA_PRIORITIES.md). |
| Build a decision tool | A simple recent + nearby report-history rule is the supported daily baseline. The CNN's five-fold geographic gain is **47.96% versus 47.11%**, with a seven-day-block interval that includes zero. | Display report-site priority with observed BMA road sites and terrain/cover/canal context. Freeze the score before evaluation, compare at the same inspection budget with current practice, and record verified findings and staff time. [Field pilot](flood_bangkok_field_pilot.py). |

## What needs new evidence first

| Research recommendation | Gate before claiming the outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Calibrated flood probability and susceptibility maps | Obtain time-matched **urban wet and dry** observations with their visible or valid area. Current complaint and positive-only satellite data do not identify false alarms or probability calibration. AUC, CSI, POD, and FAR require a defensible observed-negative/coverage definition. A high AUC on unreported cells treated as dry would be misleading. |
| Flood-depth prediction | Collect many independently located, timed depth measurements across storms and land uses. The existing BMA depth head lost to a constant 15 cm predictor on 29 matched cells in one test. Prefer depth MAE and bias on real observations; MAPE is unstable near zero depth. [Depth test](FLOOD_BANGKOK_MULTITASK_DEPTH_CNN.md). |
| Citywide 1D–2D hydraulic digital twin | The BMA LiDAR terrain exists, but verified sewer/culvert links, cross-sections, pump/gate rules, and time-matched boundary levels do not. Start with one instrumented catchment and obtain as-built and operation data. Calibrate on one set of storms and validate on later storms and observed extents before expanding. [Current evidence](FLOOD_CURRENT_STATE.md). |
| ML surrogate, PINN, hybrid simulation and ensembles | First validate a hydraulic model against independent water/depth observations. A surrogate can then imitate its simulations; an ensemble can quantify specified input/model uncertainty. Neither converts an unvalidated simulator or complaint ranker into measured flood probability. |
| Doubling-rain, intervention and cost-benefit scenarios | Current rain-conditioned models were tested against reports or proxy water, often with full-day rain that can occur after the observation. Their response to a changed rain input is a **sensitivity test**, not a verified counterfactual. Quantitative avoided-damage claims need a validated hydraulic response, exposed assets, damage curves, and uncertainty. [Scenario preparation](FLOOD_BANGKOK_SCENARIO_PREPARATION.md). |

## Recommended order of work

1. **Freeze a useful current decision:** use the transparent report-site queue and the [prospective field pilot](FLOOD_BANGKOK_PROSPECTIVE_PILOT.md). Keep its outcome name tied to reported or verified inspection sites, not all inundation.
2. **Build a reliable urban observation set:** archive timed camera or field views during new storms; mark visible wet, visible dry, and unknown ground; retain source, acquisition time, scene/frame hash, reviewer agreement, and location uncertainty. Match rain only up to each observation time. The [verified-extent plan](FLOOD_BANGKOK_VERIFIED_EXTENT_PLAN.md) describes the collection design.
3. **Run a fresh held-out evaluation:** hold out whole storms and districts; compare report history, land-cover baselines, and new models on the *same* target and candidate area. Report uncertainty and counts as well as ranking. Q4 2025 should remain exploratory rather than a new final test.
4. **Pilot physics in one catchment:** pursue the drainage as-builts and operations needed for a coupled model. Build surrogates and intervention analysis only after the physical model clears the independent event test.

The immediate constraint is **observation quality and timing**, not model size. On the current 205 weak-label scene files, the [audit output](artifacts/flood_open_weak_train/label_readiness_audit.json) confirms 145 positive-only EOS-RS/GISTDA patch-scenes and 60 UNOSAT patch-scenes with an analysis extent. These are patch-scenes across shared events, not 205 independent storms. The manifest still lacks verified acquisition hours, so none of these files alone supports pre-event rain forecasting or calibrated Bangkok street-flood probability.
